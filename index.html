Real questions.
Honest answers.

We've heard every version of "but what about…" — here's the truth.

I don't have good lighting or a camera setup. Can I still do this?+
Yes — and honestly, this kit was made for that exact situation. Everything inside assumes you're working with a phone and whatever natural light comes through your window. The creators getting the most views on Instagram right now aren't shooting in studios. Your setup isn't the problem.
What if I genuinely have no idea what to post about?+
Days 1 and 2 are only for this. There's a worksheet — takes about 10 minutes — and nobody who's done it has come out the other side still stuck. You already have things worth saying. The worksheet just helps you see them.
How much time does this actually take each day?+
30 minutes most days. On the day you shoot, maybe 45. Never more than that — the whole kit is designed around the fact that you have a real life. Plus, half the time people "spend on content" is actually just staring at a blank page. The structure takes that away.
Is this actually free? What's the catch?+
It's free. Sign up, get the kit, make three reels. That's it. The thinking is: if we show you this actually works, you'll want more — and there's more available after day 7 if you want it. But there's no pressure, no billing, no surprise charge on day 8.
What if I post and barely anyone sees it?+
That will probably happen. Every creator's first reels get low views — it's just how the algorithm works before it knows what you're about. The goal of week 1 isn't to go viral. It's to post three times, which 90% of people who "want to start" never actually do. The views come after the habit.
